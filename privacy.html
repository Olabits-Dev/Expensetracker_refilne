Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the Expense Tracker mobile application ("App") created by Olabits. The App is designed as a local utility tool to help users monitor and track their personal expenses.

1. Information Collection and Use

Expense Tracker is a fully offline application.

  • We do not collect, transmit, upload, or share any of your personal information, financial data, or transaction logs.
  • All expense records, descriptions, amounts, dates, and preferences are stored strictly and locally on your physical device using secure native sandboxed storage.
  • No internet connection is required to run the application, and the App does not make network requests to external servers.

2. Data Security

Because all data remains on your physical device, the security of your data depends on the security of your mobile device. We recommend securing your device with a PIN, pattern, password, or biometric lock to prevent unauthorized access to your local data.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party APIs, advertisements, tracking networks, analytics SDKs, or cloud services. Your data remains entirely yours and never leaves your control.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no personal information is collected, stored, or transmitted, the App is fully compliant with children's privacy regulations.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since the App is offline, we recommend reviewing this policy text periodically for any updates. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
